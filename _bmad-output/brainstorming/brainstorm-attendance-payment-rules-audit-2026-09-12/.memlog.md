- (insight) Runtime audit found five high-impact rule divergences: absence parent approval, provider holiday exemptions, licensed-only drop-ins, Dec 31 holiday lookup, and copay tiering.
- (direction) Audit rules in business-policy order: absence eligibility, holiday/drop-in eligibility, hours/rates, copay, ART fees, slots, confirmation/payout status, and data-quality handling.
- (question) Does production policy still require parent approval to gate paid absences, or is the v3 confirmation-window plus county-limit rule intentional?
- (decision) Parent confirmation does not govern absence payment because an absence has no check-in/check-out transaction for a parent to approve.
- (insight) Absence eligibility should be evaluated from scheduled authorization, zero attendance transaction/hours, holiday and care-not-offered exclusions, monthly absence capacity, and enrollment/override rules; parent confirmation belongs to recorded attendance transactions, not pure absences.
- (question) For a scheduled day with no attendance transaction, within the monthly absence limit, and not a holiday or Care Not Offered day, should the agent treat the day as payable absence automatically?
- (decision) Provider types EFACH, DEFAB, and DEFAH are exempt from paid holiday payment.
- (insight) Holiday exemption must apply consistently to regular authorization holiday code 1 and slot-contract holiday code 9, and the provider-safe result should explain the exclusion when relevant.
- (question) For licensed-only drop-in policy, should a drop-in day be paid only when the provider has an active OPEN or CLOSED license on the care date and is not EFACH, with no payment when the license status is missing or outside its effective dates?
- decision: "For licensed-only drop-in payment, provider license status must be OPEN, effective on the care date, and provider type must not be EFACH; CLOSED does not qualify."
- insight: "The drop-in eligibility branch must distinguish Universal counties from Licensed Only counties; only Licensed Only invokes the OPEN/effective/non-EFACH license gate."
- question: "For a December 31 care date, should holiday matching check the next calendar year's January 1 holiday record as well as the normal current-year record, so an observed or adjacent New Year's holiday is not missed?"
- decision: "Holiday matching for a December 31 care date must inspect the next calendar year's January 1 holiday record, including observed dates."
- insight: "Holiday matching needs explicit year-boundary handling and must still apply county holiday eligibility, provider exemption, and already-paid-on-the-paired-date checks."
- question: "For copay, should the agent implement the written FPG-based tiers: Tier 1 at or below 100% FPG, Tier 2 above 100% through 200% using the R00393 brackets/multipliers, and Tier 3 above 200% using the applicable maximum, instead of relying on the current flat authorization copay amount?"
- decision: "For the current release, the received authorization copay amount is authoritative; do not calculate FPG tiers, R00393 brackets, multipliers, or maximums in the agent."
- insight: "Copay calculation is currently a source-value application problem, not an income-policy calculation problem; preserve the received amount and apply it only to eligible regular care as defined by the source contract."
- question: "If the source supplies a copay amount of $0.00, should the agent use zero exactly, with no minimum-copay inference, unless a future source contract explicitly supplies a minimum?"
- decision: "A supplied copay amount of $0.00 is applied as zero; no minimum-copay inference is permitted without an explicit future source field or rule."
- insight: "The agent must distinguish a source-supplied zero from missing copay data; missing data should be a readiness/data-quality condition, while an explicit zero is a valid value."
- question: "For ART fees (activity, registration, transportation), should fees be charged only on regular attended care days, with no ART fee on absence, drop-in, holiday, or Care Not Offered days, as the current evaluator effectively does?"
- decision: "ART fees are charged only on regular attended care days; no ART fee is charged for absence, drop-in, holiday, or Care Not Offered days."
- insight: "ART fee eligibility is classification-driven and should be evaluated after the daily care classification; fee frequency and prior-paid history still determine whether an eligible regular day receives a fee."
- question: "For ART county and child ceilings, should the source/MCP provide an already ceiling-adjusted payable fee amount, with the evaluator applying only eligibility/frequency/history, or should the deterministic evaluator enforce county and child fiscal-year ceilings itself?"
- decision: "ART amounts are received through the authorization; the agent calculates the current month's ART fee using authorization amounts, provider ART fee details, frequency/month rules, prior paid totals, deductions, and remaining balance."
- insight: "ART calculation is a source-backed reconciliation: authorization amount is the starting entitlement, fee frequency determines whether the fee is due, payment history supplies past paid/deducted totals, and the result exposes current-month pending fee plus leftover capacity."
- question: "Should each authorization's activity, registration, and transportation amount be treated as the per-child authorized ART amount, with the provider fee-detail frequency and eligible-month fields determining when that amount is due?"
- decision: "Each authorization's activity, registration, and transportation amount is treated as the per-child authorized ART amount; fee frequency and eligible-month fields determine when it is due."
- insight: "ART must preserve fee type separately: activity, registration, and transportation each have independent authorization amounts, frequency, eligible months, prior deductions, and remaining balance."
- question: "When multiple authorizations share the same provider ART fee pool, should the agent sum the current amounts due across authorizations by fee type, subtract provider-level prior paid/deducted totals, and report both provider total pending and per-authorization contributions?"
- decision: "Each authorization's activity, registration, and transportation amount is treated as the per-child authorized ART amount; fee frequency and eligible-month fields determine when it is due."
- insight: "ART must preserve fee type separately: activity, registration, and transportation each have independent authorization amounts, frequency, eligible months, prior deductions, and remaining balance."
- question: "When multiple authorizations share the same provider ART fee pool, should the agent sum the current amounts due across authorizations by fee type, subtract provider-level prior paid/deducted totals, and report both provider total pending and per-authorization contributions?"
- decision: "ART is not a provider-level shared pool; it is a provider-defined standard fee applied independently to each eligible child authorization, subject to county permission."
- insight: "The county is an eligibility gate for ART; once allowed, the provider rate schedule supplies frequency, excluded months/days, and other conditions, while each authorization is calculated independently with its own amount, history, deductions, and leftover."
- question: "For an eligible authorization, should the agent calculate activity, registration, and transportation independently per child, subtract that child's prior paid/deducted amount for each fee type, and never reduce one child's fee because another child received the same provider-standard fee?"
- decision: "For the current ART calculation, do not rely on historical payment records; calculate the current month's ART amount from the authorization, county permission, rate schedule, frequency, excluded months/days, and other current inputs."
- insight: "Historical ART reconciliation is out of scope for now; the agent must not invent prior-paid totals, deductions, or leftover balances from unavailable payment history and should label the result as a current calculation."
- question: "Until historical payment data is intentionally added, should the ART response show only the current calculated amount due per eligible child and fee type, omitting prior-paid, deducted, and leftover fields rather than estimating them?"
- decision: "Until payment history is intentionally added, ART output shows only the current calculated amount per eligible child and fee type; prior-paid, deductions, and leftover balances are omitted."
- insight: "The Apex contract currently exposes authorization ART amounts and fiscal rate fee frequency/month fields, but the shown county response does not expose an explicit ART permission field; county ART eligibility needs a confirmed source field or contract mapping before calculation can be trusted."
- question: "What exact county source field determines whether activity, registration, and transportation ART fees are allowed, and should the agent treat missing county permission as not allowed, unavailable, or a blocked calculation?"
- decision: "The county ART standard fee fields are PAYMENT_Q10__c/activity amount, PAYMENT_Q10_1__c/activity frequency per child per year, PAYMENT_Q9__c/registration amount, PAYMENT_Q9_1__c/registration frequency per child per year, PAYMENT_Q11__c/transport amount, and PAYMENT_Q11_1__c/transport frequency per child per year."
- insight: "ART source mapping must distinguish county rate-plan standard fee amounts/frequencies from authorization and child eligibility data; these PAYMENT_Q fields are the authoritative fee schedule inputs identified by the business owner."
- question: "Are PAYMENT_Q9/Q10/Q11 fee amounts and frequencies county-rate-plan values that apply to every eligible child in that county, while authorization fields only determine whether the child is eligible and in scope?"
- decision: "PAYMENT_Q9/Q10/Q11 fee amounts and frequencies are county-rate-plan values applied independently to every eligible child in that county; authorization data determines child eligibility and scope."
- insight: "ART eligibility has two layers: county rate-plan permission and standard fee/frequency, then authorization/child eligibility; the same county fee must not be treated as a shared provider pool."
- question: "How should PAYMENT_Q9_1__c, PAYMENT_Q10_1__c, and PAYMENT_Q11_1__c frequency values be interpreted: are they counts of allowed charges per child per year, and what do values such as 1, 2, 4, and 12 mean?"

decision: County PAYMENT_Q9/Q10/Q11 values are maximum allowable ART caps by fee type; provider-level ART definitions supply the actual fee, frequency, excluded days, and related calculation rules, and provider fees may not exceed the applicable county cap.
insight: ART calculation must compare provider-defined earned/eligible charges from each authorization start date against paid amounts, while enforcing county caps as an upper bound rather than treating county PAYMENT_Q amounts as the provider's actual fee.
question: When a provider-defined ART fee exceeds the county cap, should the calculation cap it down to the county maximum, or mark the fee definition invalid and block ART for that fee type?

decision: ART calculation should use the applicable authorization ART amount per child, validate it against the provider rate schedule's allowable amount/cap, and calculate eligible earned versus paid/pending amounts using provider-defined frequency and exclusions from the authorization effective start date.
insight: County PAYMENT_Q fields remain the county-level maximum validation boundary; authorization amounts are child-specific inputs, while provider rate-schedule amount fields provide the provider-side cap/allowable amount. The calculation must not silently let a child authorization exceed either boundary.
question: Which existing rate-schedule field is the provider-side ART cap: fiscal-rate amt_provr__c, fiscal-rate-fee AMT_ACT_FA__/AMT_REG_FA__/AMT_TRANS_FA__, or another field?

decision: The provider-side ART allowable/cap amount is sourced from T_FISCAL_RAT_FEES__c.AMT_ACT_FA__c for activity, AMT_REG_FA__c for registration, and AMT_TRANS_FA__c for transportation.
insight: ART amount validation uses the authorization amount per child against the matching fiscal-rate-fee AMT_*_FA amount, with county PAYMENT_Q fields remaining the outer county maximum; frequency and exclusion rules are applied from the authorization effective start date.
question: Which source fields define provider ART frequency and excluded days, and are those exclusions specific to each ART fee type or shared across activity, registration, and transportation?

decision: Provider ART fee schedule exclusion months are read from TXT_ACT_MONTH__c, TXT_REG_MONTH__c, and TXT_TRANS_MONTH__c as semicolon-delimited month numbers 1 through 12; the matching ART fee type is not payable in those months.
decision: Provider ART frequency codes are CDE_ACT_FREQ__c, CDE_REG_FREQ__c, and CDE_TRANS_FREQ__c, with ANN meaning annually, MTH meaning monthly, and ONE meaning one time.
insight: ART eligibility is fee-type-specific: activity, registration, and transportation each use their own AMT_*_FA cap, CDE_*_FREQ frequency, and TXT_*_MONTH excluded-month list, then compare authorization amounts and paid amounts from the authorization start date.
question: For ANN and ONE ART frequencies, what is the triggering date/window from DTE_BEGIN_EFFV_AUTH__c: ANN once per calendar year or once per authorization year, and ONE immediately/once after authorization start or on a specific month/date?

decision: ANN ART frequency means one eligible charge per authorization year anchored to DTE_BEGIN_EFFV_AUTH__c; the annual charge is not eligible when its applicable month is listed in the matching TXT_*_MONTH exclusion field, including when the authorization-year calculation overlaps an excluded month.
insight: Annual ART must be evaluated on authorization-year boundaries rather than calendar-year boundaries, with exclusion-month suppression applied before earned/pending totals are calculated.
question: For ONE ART frequency, is the single eligible charge due immediately at authorization start, or only when the authorization reaches a specific month/date, also subject to the matching excluded-month list?

decision: ONE ART frequency means one eligible charge in the first non-excluded month after DTE_BEGIN_EFFV_AUTH__c; the matching TXT_ACT_MONTH__c, TXT_REG_MONTH__c, or TXT_TRANS_MONTH__c exclusion list is applied before eligibility.
insight: ART timing is now defined for all frequencies: MTH once per non-excluded month, ANN once per authorization year anchored to authorization start, and ONE once in the first non-excluded month after authorization start.
question: What exact county rate-plan field indicates whether ART is allowed/permitted for the fee type, and what should happen when that permission field is false or missing?

decision: County ART cap mapping is PAYMENT_Q10__c for Activity (A), PAYMENT_Q9__c for Registration (R), and PAYMENT_Q11__c for Transportation (T).
insight: County PAYMENT_Q ART fields are fee-type-specific maximum validation values; they must be matched to the corresponding authorization amount and provider fiscal-rate-fee cap before calculating earned, paid, or pending ART.
question: Should a zero or blank county ART cap mean that the fee type is not permitted, and should the system block that ART type rather than calculate it?

assumption: For now, a zero or blank county ART cap for Activity, Registration, or Transportation means that fee type is not permitted; block that fee type from ART calculation while continuing other permitted fee types.
insight: The county ART cap is a permission and maximum boundary: a positive cap permits validation up to that amount, while zero or missing blocks the matching ART type pending confirmation.
question: For paid ART matching, should paid amounts be identified from payment-detail fields amt_act_paid__c, amt_reg_paid__c, and amt_trans_paid__c, matched by authorization and care date, with only paid records on or after DTE_BEGIN_EFFV_AUTH__c reducing the calculated earned ART?
decision: "Current implementation calculates earned ART only; payment data is not currently available, so paid ART defaults to zero and pending ART equals earned ART."
insight: "Keep paid ART as an explicit future input/reconciliation layer. It must not influence current eligibility, timing, or earned-amount calculation until payment-detail data is integrated."
question: "For a permitted ART type, should the eligible amount per charge be the minimum of the authorization amount, provider fiscal-rate-fee amount, and positive county cap, with the fee type blocked when any required source amount is blank or zero?"
